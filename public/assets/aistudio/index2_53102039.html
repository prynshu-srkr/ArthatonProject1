Golden UPI Rule #1 • Collect Requests

You NEVER need to enter your UPI PIN or scan a QR code to receive money. Entering your PIN always deducts money from your bank account.

Golden UPI Rule #2 • KYC & APK Phishing

Banks and NPCI never ask you to update PAN/KYC or unblock accounts via SMS/WhatsApp links or screen-sharing apps like AnyDesk.

Golden UPI Rule #3 • Golden Hour Reporting

Victim of a UPI or banking fraud? Call the National Cyber Crime Helpline at 1930 or report at cybercrime.gov.in within the golden hour to freeze stolen funds.

Call 1930 Helpline Now Report on cybercrime.gov.in
1

Turn OFF Mobile Data & Wi-Fi (Airplane Mode)

If you clicked an unknown link or installed an .apk / AnyDesk app, cut internet access immediately so scammers cannot read your SMS OTPs.

• यदि आपने किसी लिंक पर क्लिक किया है या कोई ऐप डाउनलोड किया है, तो तुरंत मोबाइल डेटा और Wi-Fi बंद करें।

2

Dial 1930 Within the "Golden Hour" to Freeze Money

Keep the 12-digit UPI Reference / UTR number and screenshot ready. Reporting within 15–60 minutes lets Cyber Police freeze the scammer's bank account before withdrawal.

• पैसे कटने पर तुरंत 1930 पर कॉल करें और UTR नंबर बताएं ताकि ठग के बैंक खाते में रकम फ्रीज़ की जा सके।

3

Block Your UPI ID & Change MPIN / NetBanking Password

Open your official bank app (or call your bank's official toll-free number on the back of your debit card) to temporarily block UPI and cards.

• अपने बैंक ऐप से UPI ID को अस्थायी रूप से ब्लॉक करें और अपना UPI पिन/पासवर्ड बदल लें।

4

Save Evidence (Do Not Delete the Message)

Take screenshots of the SMS/WhatsApp message, sender number, UPI ID, and bank debit alert for your 1930 cybercrime complaint.

• सबूत के तौर पर SMS, फोन नंबर और ट्रांज़ैक्शन का स्क्रीनशॉट सुरक्षित रखें, संदेश को डिलीट न करें।
